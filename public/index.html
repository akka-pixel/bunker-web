PSG Bunker - Smart Attendance Tracker & Bunk Planner for PSG Tech

Welcome to PSG Bunker (psg-bunk.vercel.app), the definitive attendance tracking utility and smart bunk planner engineered exclusively for students of PSG College of Technology (PSG Tech) and PSG Institute of Advanced Studies (PSG IAS), Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu.

Why PSG Tech Students Choose PSG Bunker

PSG Bunker provides direct, zero-friction synchronization with the PSG Tech eCampus portal (ecampus.psgtech.ac.in/studzone). Built specifically around the PSG autonomous curriculum, Bunker helps students monitor their hourly attendance records, plan bunks within safe regulatory limits, check internal assessment marks (CA tests), tutorial scores, and view dynamic day-wise lecture timetables.

PSG Tech 75% Attendance Rule & Safe Bunk Calculator Formula

At PSG College of Technology, attendance is calculated on a strict period-by-period (hourly) basis. Every hour attended directly influences your eligibility for continuous assessments and end-semester examinations: Attendance Percentage = (Total Hours Attended / Total Hours Conducted) × 100

  • Safe Bunk Formula: If your attendance is ≥ 75%, PSG Bunker calculates: Max Bunks = floor((Attended Hours - 0.75 × Conducted Hours) / 0.75). This tells you the exact number of hours you can skip without falling below the 75% boundary.
  • Recovery Formula: If your attendance is < 75%, PSG Bunker calculates: Required Consecutive Hours = ceil((0.75 × Conducted Hours - Attended Hours) / 0.25). This reveals how many consecutive lectures you must attend to bounce back to 75%.
  • Condonation Zone: Students between 65% and 74.9% fall into condonation and require authorized medical leave certificates. Below 65% requires repeating the course.

Supported Branches & Programmes at PSG Tech

PSG Bunker supports all undergraduate (B.E., B.Tech), postgraduate (M.E., M.Tech, MCA, MBA), and integrated M.Sc./B.Sc. programmes: Automobile Engineering, Biomedical, Biotechnology, Civil, Computer Science and Engineering (CSE), AI & Machine Learning (AIML), Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE), Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE), Information Technology (IT), Instrumentation and Control (ICE), Mechanical Engineering, Metallurgical Engineering, Production Engineering, Robotics and Automation, Fashion Technology, and Textile Technology.

Key Features of PSG Bunker Web App

  • Live eCampus Sync: Instantly fetch up-to-date attendance statistics with a single tap.
  • Safe Bunk Calculator: Color-coded indicators showing exactly how many classes you can afford to miss.
  • Continuous Assessment (CA) Marks: View CA 1, CA 2, CA 3, and assignment tutorial marks categorized by course codes.
  • GPA & CGPA Insights: Comprehensive semester-wise SGPA and cumulative CGPA historical records.
  • Academic Calendar & Schedule: Dynamic interactive timeline featuring semester opening dates, cycle tests, model exams, laboratory examinations, and college holidays.
  • Daily Timetable Tracker: Automatic timetable mapping based on student section and semester schedule.
  • Offline Progressive Web App (PWA): Save PSG Bunker to your Android or iPhone home screen with offline cached data access.
